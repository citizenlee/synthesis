# Storylines

Three framings for the same 90–120 s film. Each uses Story house style (sections 1–3 of `references/style-analysis.md`): short declaratives, 3–7 word lines, name late, hinge at 28–60%, a triplet, poem register. Draft VO is for rewrite. Nothing here is a specification.

**Default:** A, which is what `outline.md` cuts. B is the IBBIS landscape film. C is the policy lock.

Hard constraints on every framing:

1. Film, not product. No factory exists in this repo.
2. No hazardous sequences, no misuse, no bypass. Safeguards are locks.
3. Flag unverified lines. Do not import Cultivarium positioning.

---

## A. Every plate takes its own route *(default)*

**Logline:** High-mix nucleic acid synthesis needs routing, not a fixed line — every plate its own path, different sequences without a changeover.

**Hinge line:** "Every plate takes its own route." *(~31% in the 110 s cut)*

**Why this one:** It is the argument the repo already makes (CircuitHub transposed). ANVIL's committed clips already picture it. Least invention.

**Risk:** "96 different sequences / no changeover" is overlay copy, not a spec. Speak "different sequences" unless someone verifies a number. "Built here" is register, not a plant.

### Draft VO (poem, ~40 words)

```
Orders used to wait.
One plate. One line.
Then a changeover.

Every plate takes its own route.

Pumps.
Plates.
Routes.

Different sequences.
No changeover.

No verified screen.
No synthesis.
The run leaves a record.

Built here.
```

Company name: end card only. Optional spoken last word: the chosen name, once, after a 2 s pause — only if the name is decided.

### Triplet (optional, if the cut needs to hit ~30 wpm)

At 0:42–0:52, over pumps / head / fill: "Pumps. / Plates. / Routes."

### Closing card

Small wordmark. One line: "Every plate takes its own route." No domain until it exists.

## B. The factory fits on a bench *(landscape)*

Uses the IBBIS Nov 2026 preliminary findings (`research/benchtop-synthesis-landscape/`). Devices proliferating; array chemistry approaching mail-order scale; DNA leaving the catalog for the bench. Stats stay off-screen unless attributed, and even then prefer qualitative.

**Logline:** Benchtop synthesizers are multiplying, array chemistry is catching up to the catalog, and DNA is becoming a thing a room can make.

**Hinge line:** "Then the factory fit on a bench." *(place at ~40%)*

**Why this one:** It is the only framing that needs the new research. It is a landscape film, not a product film — honest, given nothing is built.

**Risk:** IBBIS numbers (9.3 models/year, 1.5 billion unique nucleotides, 4,350,000 sites, China 31.8% / USA 25%) will try to get into the VO. They should not, unless a card literally cites IBBIS. Do not turn "decentralized DNA" into a how-to. Screening, if it appears, stays a positive lock (per-device authorization, audit log) so proliferation is not a thriller.

### Draft VO (poem)

```
The catalog used to be far away.
A bench. A room. A hall.
Devices, then more devices.

Then the factory fit on a bench.

Array, well, column.
Many sites. One run.
Mail-order scale, at home.

A screen on the device.
A key for this machine.
A record of the run.

The bench is the factory.
```

"Mail-order scale, at home" **[IBBIS: "throughput now approaches scales previously only available from mail-order synthesis providers"]** — qualitative, attributed in the live intro if used, not as our spec.

### Triplet

"A bench. / A room. / A hall." (device formats, IBBIS Fig. 1A) — already in the draft.

### Closing card

"The bench is the factory." Wordmark late. Optional small credit: "Landscape: IBBIS, Nov 2026."

## C. No verified screen. No synthesis. *(the lock)*

**Logline:** Screening used to sit with the supplier. The policy puts it in the machine — per-device authorization, a signed job, a record of the run.

**Hinge line:** "So the screen lives in the machine." *(~36%)*

**Why this one:** NSTM-6 is the film's stated subject in the root README. The October 2027 clause (benchtops refuse a run without a verified Screener authorization) is the sharpest sentence in the repo.

**Risk:** Looks like a compliance ad. Keep CircuitHub understatement. Never show a rejected "bad" sequence (that is a specific sequence). Never show tamper-as-plot. "Adherent by design" is ANVIL end-card copy, not a certification we hold — card only, or cut.

### Draft VO (poem)

```
A checkbox used to be enough.
A supplier. A form. A shipment.

So the screen lives in the machine.

A key for this device.
A job that has to be signed.
A record that stays.

No verified screen.
No synthesis.
```

### Triplet

"A key for this device. / A job that has to be signed. / A record that stays." — KYC-adjacent / per-device authorization / signed jobs / audit logs, in nouns, no jargon.

### Closing card

"No verified screen. No synthesis." Optional second line: "NSTM-6." No HHS-list claim.
